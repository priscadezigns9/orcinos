OPTIONAL PLAYER MATCH FEATURES

Wagers and RPS Chips

In eligible player-versus-player games, each player can choose an optional wager from 200 to 1,000 RPS Chips, in 100-Chip increments. The winner takes the pot. A draw or tied top score returns the stakes, and cancelling before the first move charges no stake.

RPS Chips are virtual in-game currency and have no cash value. Bot practice does not use wagers.
SPECIAL MOVES

Use them at the right moment

Special moves are available in authenticated player matches. Only one special action can be used in a turn.

QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

Can I practice without signing in?

Yes. Guest practice is a local bot match and does not save results or rewards.

Do RPS Chips have cash value?

No. RPS Chips are virtual in-game currency and have no cash value.

What happens to a wager if the match is drawn?

When the match ends in a draw or tied top score, the stakes are returned. Cancelling before the first move charges no stake.

How do I join an online game?

Sign in and join an open game, or join a private match with its room code.
