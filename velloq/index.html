Zero-Cost Scaling

No monthly per-vehicle fees.

Scale your fleet from 5 vehicles to 500 without watching a meter run. Velloq's architecture eliminates the per-vehicle subscription model that drains operational budgets. One deployment. Infinite expansion. Your data stays yours — no metered access, no vendor lock-in, no surprise invoices when you add a new truck to the road.

Real-Time Diagnostics

Pull Fault Codes, Fuel, and Engine Vitals.

Every vehicle is a live telemetry node. Velloq streams OBD-II fault codes, fuel levels, engine temperature, RPM, and battery health directly to your dashboard in real time. Catch a failing alternator before it strands a driver on the Solomon Hochoy Highway. Diagnose, triage, and dispatch — all from a single command center, without waiting for a monthly vendor report.

Professional Reporting

PDF/Excel audits ready for the boardroom.

Generate boardroom-grade fleet audits in seconds. Velloq compiles telemetry, fuel consumption, maintenance logs, and driver behavior into polished PDF and Excel exports — formatted for stakeholders, insurers, and compliance officers. No manual spreadsheet wrangling. No third-party reporting tools. Your data, your format, your authority — delivered on demand.
