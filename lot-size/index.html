Swap is displayed in broker points, not USD. The screenshot gives the swap rates, but converting them into dollar holding costs requires the current instrument/contract calculation. I show the rates so you don’t have to enter or guess them; they are not added to the stop-risk estimate.

Formula: risk ÷ (stop distance × pip value + the single listed commission charge), rounded down to the account’s lot step. Instant is marked “in-deals”; Competition is marked “out-deals”, so each listed rate is counted once.
